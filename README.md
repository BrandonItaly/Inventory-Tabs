# Inventory Tabs Next

A recreation of Inventory Tabs for newer versions of Minecraft, available for Fabric and NeoForge.

This mod adds a row of tabs above your inventory and container screens. Easily switch to nearby chests, crafting tables, furnaces, and other containers with a click or a key press without leaving your inventory.

![tabs preview](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/9ERgRKUo/images/1ec503cc525bf6b61f8686992c479f156458a527.png)

## Features
- Switch between nearby chests, crafting tables, furnaces, and other containers without leaving your inventory.
- Swap to nearby entity inventories, including chest boats, chest minecarts, villagers, and horses.
- Access your full inventory while riding horses or other entities, without dismounting.
  
  ![full inventory access while riding an entity](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/9ERgRKUo/images/d9d12cb5fdfedbf5dfc593593b558b0769e0d4bc.png)
- Carried item stacks carry over to the next screen as long as you have an empty inventory slot.
